# Mission

Delta Health helps veterans organize and track everything related to their VA and
community care: appointments, history, notes, medications, rescheduled visits,
doctors, offices, and contact info — what they were seen for, what happened, and
what the next action items are.

The goal: give veterans a one-stop, organized, automated way to hold the VA
accountable and get the care they were promised, without carrying it all in their
head or across a dozen disconnected portals.

## Approved reference sources only

Content, links, and factual claims in this app pull ONLY from:

**Official VA policy & rights**
- VA Patient Bill of Rights — va.gov
- VA MISSION Act & Community Care rules — va.gov
- VA Facility & Urgent Care Finder — va.gov
- PACT Act Benefits Dashboard — va.gov

**Trustworthy medical/clinical reference**
- MedlinePlus (National Library of Medicine) — medlineplus.gov
- Mayo Clinic Health Library — mayoclinic.org
- Cleveland Clinic Health Library — clevelandclinic.org
- CDC — cdc.gov
- American Cancer Society (testicular cancer resources) — cancer.org

**Alternative medicine scientific analysis**
- NIH Homeopathy Fact Sheet — nih.gov

Do not cite, link, or scrape any other source without updating this list first.
See `src/lib/approvedSources.ts` for the enforced URL list used in code.
